Bop Desktop 0.1.8 — a bubble you can actually move, a picture you can choose, sections that switch
=================================================================================================
Round reported (from the packaged 0.1.7 the user was running):

  "the chat bubble cannot be moved freely at all; when I open a space, the other sections like Guide or
   Settings or Alerts do not respond — I have to press ✕ to leave the space first, which is a nuisance,
   let me switch freely between all sections; also make the bubble's avatar icon a bit nicer and let me
   design/add my own picture to it; and when the app is full screen these spaces should not huddle up
   like that — let them compress or expand so it looks better."

All four were measurable, and the first one measured differently from the report: the drag did work, but
only if the pointer happened to land on a ~13px ring, and it arrived short.

  1. The bubble's drag, with the machine's real pointer
  ----------------------------------------------------
  Measured on 0.1.7 first, with tools/drag-check.js (SetCursorPos + mouse_event, i.e. a real press, real
  moves, real release over the real window — a synthetic DOM event cannot prove anything here, because
  the window is moved by the operating system):

      press (rim) 43,393 · asked 260x150 · window 38,361 -> 265,492
      moved 227,131  =>  33 points short of the hand that dragged it

  And the surface: `-webkit-app-region: drag` covered the 60px box while the 38px round button in the
  middle was `no-drag`, so the only part of a 64px bubble that moved the window was a ring about 13px
  wide. A press that looks like a grab lands on the button — which opens the menu, exactly what the
  user's own screenshot shows.

  Fix: the renderer reports the pointer (pointerdown/move/up with screenX/screenY and pointer capture) and
  main places the window, one point per point, through src/main/bubble-drag.js. A press that travels less
  than 4 points is still a click, so the menu is as easy to open as before.

  Measured on 0.1.8, same tool, pressing the CENTRE of the bubble (where a hand aims):

      source build     press 70,393 · before 38,361 · after 258,491 · asked +220,+130 · offBy 0
      portable EXE     press 70,393 · before 38,361 · after 258,491 · asked +220,+130 · offBy 0
      dev run          -X1 43 -Y1 393 -X2 343 -Y2 513   => moved dx 300 dy 120 of 300x120, offBy 0

  Size unchanged by the drag (64x64), the pointer was put back where it was, and the stored spot
  (0.134375 x 0.475775 of the 1920x1032 work area) resolves to 258,491 — the point the pointer ended on.

  A tap still behaves: smoke step bubble-tap-still-clicks {at:[1810,942], after:[1810,942], moved:false,
  menu:true} — a press that does not travel neither moves the bubble nor stops opening the space menu.

  2. Sections switch while a space is open
  ---------------------------------------
  Reproduced: nav clicks changed the page *behind* the open space (`.space-open .main { visibility:
  hidden }`), so Settings/Guide/Alerts looked dead until the ✕ was pressed. A nav click now leaves the
  space open in the background and shows the section; the space keeps its session, its page and its
  monitor.

                                            paneX:320}
                                   after  {open:null, panes:4, monitoring:"observing-auth-unknown",
                                            paneX:-20000}
                                   page settings · space-open false · pane still rendering
      live check (portable EXE)    {page:"settings", spaceOpen:false, text:"Settings\nSmall tweaks…"},
                                   paneText:"2 studies available"

  3. The bubble's face
  -------------------
  The default is now a drawn vector face in a soft-shaded sphere (bubble.css, the page's own file: the
  old inline <style> was one CSP change away from being dropped, which is what had already happened to
  the space menu). Any picture can be chosen in Settings → Floating bubble → Bubble picture:

      smoke bubble-avatar-decoded  source: a real 1264x781 PNG on disk -> pixels.kept 256 (a centred
                                   square, never stretched), data URL 35 654 characters (~26 KB)
      smoke bubble-avatar-shown    {shown:true, drawn:true, loaded:true}  (naturalWidth > 0: the page's
                                   own `img-src 'self' data:` policy allowed it)
      smoke settings-avatar        {image:true, back:true}  — preview plus "Use the drawn face"
      smoke bubble-avatar-cleared  {shown:false, drawn:true, stored:0}

  The picture is stored as a data URL inside Bop's own settings file, not as a path, so moving or deleting
  the original cannot break it; it is cropped to the largest centred square, kept at most 256px, and
  retried at 128px before it is refused. An oversized or unreadable file is refused with words, and the
  refusal is shown in Settings (avatar.test.js covers the rules, including that the two size bounds in
  avatar.js and spaces.js agree).

  4. The spaces grid uses the window it is given
  ---------------------------------------------
  Reproduced from the user's screenshot: at 1568px wide the three cards sat in the first three of nine
  `auto-fill` tracks and the rest of the window stayed empty, because `auto-fill` reserves the tracks
  nothing landed in. `auto-fit` collapses them.

      smoke spaces-grid-grows  content 1000px: card 296px, row 605 of a 605px grid
                               content 1860px: card 733px, row 1480 of a 1480px grid
                               (asserted: cards grow with the window, and every row fills >= 90% of its
                               grid)

Known limits of this round
--------------------------
  - No account was signed in anywhere; the fixtures are local pages on 127.0.0.1.
  - The drag was verified with a mouse pointer. A touch or pen gesture was not measured, and the display
    used is 100% scaled, where screen points and pixels are the same number; a 125% display was not tested.
  - Multi-monitor dragging was not measured. `workAreaFor` was corrected to read the window's own position
    (it used to look up a work area with work-area-relative coordinates), but no second display was attached.
  - The picture dialog itself (the OS picker) was not automated. Everything after it was: decode, crop,
    store, render, preview and clear.
  - A drag whose pointer leaves the 64px window is covered (every measured drag travelled 130-300 points
    outside it, and pointer capture keeps the events coming), but a drag that leaves the *screen* is not.
  - The NSIS installer target was not rebuilt, so 0.1.8 exists as a portable file only.
  - The file is not code-signed, so SmartScreen warns on first launch.
