## What none of this proves

- No real Messenger, TikTok, WhatsApp or Telegram account was signed in. Message delivery into a space
  is still unverified; the fixtures are local pages on 127.0.0.1.
- Service-worker `showNotification` and Web Push are still not captured: Electron does not implement
  the Push API, so a site that only notifies from its worker cannot be seen.
- The installer target was not rebuilt and was not installed.
- The artifact is unsigned: Windows SmartScreen warns on first launch.
