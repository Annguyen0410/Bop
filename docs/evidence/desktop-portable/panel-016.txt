Limits
------
- No panel account was signed in: the sign-in page is the proof that the site now serves the pane at all,
  not the proof of a working logged-in session.
- Only this one panel was measured (it is the panel the user reported). Other panels may have their own
  rules; the agent Bop sends is now a truthful Chrome agent, which is the best claim this engine can make.
- The refusal note was verified with a local fixture (smoke step browser-refusal-note) and unit tests,
  not with a second real refusal page.
